Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Nakodu has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). These standards apply to everything on Nakodu: posts, comments, images, profiles, chats and messages to moderators.

What's prohibited

  • Any content that sexualises minors, whether real, drawn, or generated.
  • Grooming: contacting or building a relationship with a minor for sexual purposes.
  • Sharing, requesting or linking to CSAM, or trading it in any way.
  • Sextortion, or threatening to share intimate images of a minor.

How we prevent it

  • Nakodu is not for anyone under 13; everyone gives their birthday when they sign up, and 18+ content needs a birthday showing they're an adult.
  • Private messages start as chat requests, which the other person must accept, and anyone can be blocked at any time.
  • Every post, comment, profile and chat can be reported from inside the app.
  • Admins and moderators can remove content and ban accounts across the whole site.

How to report

  • In the app, tap Report on the post, comment or profile.
  • Or email support@nakodu.top with a link to the content or the username. Please don't attach the material itself.
  • If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local police first.

What we do

  • We review reports of child safety issues first, ahead of everything else.
  • We remove the content and ban the accounts involved.
  • We report CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and cooperate with law enforcement as required by law.
  • We preserve evidence as required by law for investigations.

Contact for child safety

Our designated child safety contact can be reached at support@nakodu.top.
