Delete your Nakodu account

Last updated: 30 October 2026

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Nakodu and log in.
  2. Open the menu, then Settings, then Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away. This can't be undone.

Without the app

Email support@nakodu.top from any address with the subject "Delete my account" and your username. Because Nakodu accounts have no email, we'll ask you to prove the account is yours, for example by posting a short code we send you, or by sending a chat message to u/nakodu from the account. We delete the account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Deleted: your username, password, profile picture, settings, votes, saved and hidden posts, follows, chats, blocks and push notification tokens.

Kept, without your name: posts and comments you didn't delete stay visible with the author shown as "[deleted]", so conversations still make sense. Delete them first if you want them gone.

Backups: copies in nightly backups are removed within 14 days.
